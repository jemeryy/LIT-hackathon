Your checklist
Start with item 1. Keep this page as you work through the list.
1. Send a written request for the deposit
Your biggest gap. Give him 7 days to reply. The draft uses only your facts and your agreement.
Open draft
2. Gather what is missing
Stamp duty certificate, signed inventory list, final bill, dated move-in photos.
Open checklist
3. Pre-filing assessment on CJTS
Pick the category, enter 15 Aug 2026 and $2,600, answer the questions. The ID it gives you lasts 7 days.
Open CJTS
4. Fill the claim form and pay
Six parts: you, him, the claim, a summary of at most 500 characters, documents (PDF only, 5MB each, with page numbers), money order $2,600. Singpass login. Fee $10 up to $5,000. Then pick a Consultation date.
Guide s.12
5. Serve him within 7 working days
Save the Respondent Copy from CJTS, hand it to him or send by registered post, then file the Declaration of Service.
Guide p.11
6. Before the Consultation
He may open e-Negotiation (5 rounds). Fill the Submission for Hearing form: events in date order, witnesses. Bring E1 to E6 in evidence sheet order.
Guide s.21, s.36
